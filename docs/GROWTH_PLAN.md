# ZENTRIX AI — Audience-Building Playbook

A zero-budget, student-first plan for introducing ZENTRIX AI to learners and earning trust through useful demonstrations.

## Who we are trying to reach

Start with one clear audience: secondary-school and college students who want to understand mathematics, practise before tests and check their working.

Do not try to market every feature to everyone. Lead with one promise:

> Understand the steps, practise the skill, and learn from your mistakes.

## The three content pillars

### 1. Solve it with ZENTRIX
Short screen recordings showing one question, the steps and a quick check.

Example questions:
- Solve: 3x + 7 = 22
- Factorise: x² - 5x + 6
- Find the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 7 cm
- Differentiate: x⁴ + 3x²

Use questions that have been tested in the current app. Show the exact input and output. Never edit a result to hide an error.

### 2. Learn the idea
Explain one small concept in 20–40 seconds:
- Why does a negative sign change when subtracting?
- What does the gradient of a graph mean?
- Mean vs median
- Why does a quadratic have two roots?
- When can you divide both sides by a variable?

End by showing how a student can practise that idea in ZENTRIX.

### 3. Build in public
Show genuine product improvements:
- A new feature being tested
- A bug that was fixed
- A before/after interface improvement
- A student suggestion that led to a change

Be honest about limitations. Do not claim perfect accuracy or that every topic is supported.

## First 7 days

| Day | Post | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce ZENTRIX and its free student mission | 20-second screen recording |
| 2 | Solve 3x + 7 = 22 step by step | Short demo |
| 3 | Explain one common algebra mistake | Short lesson |
| 4 | Show the Exam Centre and practice flow | Screen recording |
| 5 | Demonstrate the Definitions library | Carousel or screen recording |
| 6 | Ask students what topic they struggle with most | Poll or question post |
| 7 | Share one improvement made from feedback | Build-in-public update |

Repeat the cycle with different questions and topics. Consistency matters more than posting every day.

## Ready-to-use launch post

**Meet ZENTRIX AI — a free maths learning workspace.**

The goal is simple: help students understand the method, practise questions and prepare for exams.

You can explore step-by-step solving, practice questions, an Exam Centre, searchable definitions and graphing tools.

I'm building it in public and improving it with real student feedback.

Try it, test it with a question you understand, and tell me what worked or what needs fixing.

**Mathematics Without Limits.**

## 20-second video script

"Still stuck on algebra? Let's try this: 3x + 7 = 22.

Open ZENTRIX AI, enter the equation and follow the working. Subtract 7 from both sides, then divide by 3. So x equals 5.

Don't just copy the answer — understand why each step works. ZENTRIX AI is being built to make maths practice easier for students."

Before publishing, record the actual current app and confirm the result matches the script.

## A repeatable weekly routine

- Publish 3 short educational demonstrations.
- Publish 1 feature or product update.
- Ask 1 clear feedback question.
- Spend 10–15 minutes replying helpfully to genuine comments.
- Record common questions and bugs in a simple notes file.

## Where to share

Choose only communities that allow educational project posts:
- Student and mathematics communities on Reddit
- Relevant Facebook student groups
- Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
- Your own GitHub repository and project updates
- Relevant school, college or university communities where self-promotion is permitted

Read each community's rules first. Do not mass-DM strangers, spam links or post the same message repeatedly.

## Measure progress

Track these once a week:
- Views and reach
- Profile visits
- Clicks to the project
- People who actually try a solver question
- Useful feedback and bug reports
- Returning users, if the app can measure this without collecting unnecessary personal data

Early on, meaningful feedback and repeat use matter more than follower count.

## Safety and trust

- Do not publish your home address, phone number, school timetable or private identity documents.
- Do not ask users to send passwords, OTPs, payment details or private school records.
- Use demo questions rather than exposing a student's personal homework photo.
- Do not buy followers, engagement or advertisements.
- Do not promise guaranteed grades, perfect answers or unsupported features.
- Keep the core learning tools free as promised.

## The first success milestone

Aim for 10 real students to try one question, 5 to give useful feedback, and 3 to return and use ZENTRIX again. These are learning milestones, not guaranteed growth forecasts.
