AI assists with language; it does not replace the math engine.

Gemini turns a natural-language request into a supported command.
2. Calculate
SymPy performs the mathematical operation locally.
3. Check
Independent checks are shown when available. Not every result has a formal proof.

ZENTRIX AI — Mathematics Without Limits.

ZENTRIX AI is an intelligent mathematical platform built to transform the way people solve, explore and understand mathematics. Combining symbolic computation, numerical verification and intelligent learning tools, it brings an extensive mathematical workspace into one beautifully designed application. From arithmetic and algebra to advanced calculus, differential equations, matrices, statistics, probability, geometry and physics, ZENTRIX supports learners at every level. Its step-by-step solutions explain the reasoning behind answers instead of simply displaying a result. Interactive graph visualisation, Matrix Lab, formula references, photo question recognition and a dedicated Learning Studio make complex concepts easier to explore. Math Arcade adds enjoyable quizzes, logic puzzles and mathematical mini-games, with adaptive difficulty based on recorded performance. The Hybrid Tutor provides offline explanations by default and can optionally use Gemini for more tailored guidance when connected. Mathematical computation remains local and does not require a paid AI service. Student progress stays on the device, and core mathematical tools work without an internet connection. Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, ZENTRIX is purpose-built for mathematics, combining computation, checking and learning in one place.
More than a calculator. More than an AI assistant. A complete environment for mathematical discovery.
