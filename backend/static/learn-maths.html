1. Solve a linear equation

Example: find x when 3x + 7 = 22.

3x + 7 = 22
  1. Subtract 7 from both sides: 3x = 15.
  2. Divide both sides by 3: x = 5.
  3. Check: 3(5) + 7 = 22, so the answer works.

Remember: An equation stays balanced when you perform the same operation on both sides.

2. Factorise a quadratic

Example: factorise x² − 5x + 6.

x² − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3)

Look for two numbers that multiply to +6 and add to −5. Those numbers are −2 and −3.

Check by expanding: (x − 2)(x − 3) = x² − 3x − 2x + 6 = x² − 5x + 6.

3. Understand a derivative

For a power of x, the power rule says to multiply by the exponent, then reduce the exponent by one.

If f(x) = x³, then f′(x) = 3x².

For example, at x = 2 the derivative is 3 × 2² = 12. This is the slope of the tangent to the curve at that point.
